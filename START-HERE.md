# Start here

This is a curated public research specification with original synthetic examples. The algorithm is not implemented, trained or benchmarked yet.

1. Read README.md and [ENGINEERING.md](ENGINEERING.md) for the research question, status and standing engineering rules.
2. Read [requirements](docs/01-product-requirements.md) and [architecture](docs/02-architecture.md) to inspect the proposed boundaries and data contracts.
3. Inspect the [synthetic examples](examples/README.md). Expected outcomes are authored specifications, not results from a working detector.
4. Read the [evaluation protocol](docs/04-evaluation.md) before proposing models or interpreting metrics. Preserve label availability, entity/time separation and reviewer-budget rules.
5. Use [public milestones](docs/03-build-plan.md) and [definition of done](docs/05-definition-of-done.md) to understand the remaining engineering work. Detailed internal task schedules are maintained separately.
6. Run the documentation commands in README.md. They validate supplied files and fixture consistency; they do not execute the future application or measure detection quality.
7. Review [sources](docs/06-sources-and-design-rationale.md), [publication boundaries](docs/08-publication.md) and [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Keep customer records, operational fraud thresholds, credentials and private commercial plans out of public contributions. Preserve explicit unknown outcomes and human review; a risk signal is not proof of wrongdoing.
